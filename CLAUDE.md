# Trade Shark — notes for Claude

- **Single seller, fun collector shop.** This is one person's cute card shop (tone: light, friendly, never investment talk). Don't add seller accounts, teams, logins, roles, or "sell on our platform" copy. Behind the scenes three partners (Matthew, Adrian, Mike) own the cards and get paid out (see Partners); that is admin-only, and public pages still speak as one shop ("my shop"), never imply other sellers. The only player identity is the reveal game's `Buyer` (a Stripe customer + saved card, signed `ts_player` cookie, no password); the only subscription is the optional membership. A future multi-seller split may change the folder layout; don't build toward it.
- **Packs only; marketplaces never post.** The shop sells three products: Baseball Pack, Football Pack, Pokemon Pack (`lib/categories.ts`; no basketball, no Magic, one tier each). Cards never sell as singles. On upload (`lib/publish.ts`, run from `processBatch`): no name or no photo → hold in Inbox (never invent a name; a filename guess isn't a name); $5 and under → stock (`Priced`); over $5, still sideways, or the same scan twice → **Needs a look** (`NeedsLook`) until the owner approves it in `/admin/queue` (approve = back to stock). The $5 line is dollars, not confidence. Nothing ever posts to eBay, TCGplayer or anywhere else: exports are draft CSVs the owner publishes by hand.
- **The reveal game is the only checkout** (`lib/game/*`, `/packs/[category]`, `/api/play/*`). Rules sentence (`RULES_LINE`) and odds (`oddsLines`) are on screen before the first payment. A saved card is required (Stripe Checkout setup mode via `/play/card`). $1 reveal is charged first, then a ready pack is reserved and all 12 cards are shown with engine prices; 30 s server deadline (+3 s grace), nudge (a small pack tilt) at 10 s and 20 s, never the screen. Keep = $2.99 more ($3.99), no shipping. Pass / timer / leaving the page (sendBeacon) expires the pack for that player (cards back to stock, redrawn), never charges, and locks that category for the account and its card fingerprint until the player's local midnight (`Buyer.tz`). While locked the only action is the $4.99 blind pack (no shipping): charge first, reserve from the same queue, reveal, no reject; a keep or blind buy clears the lock. One active cycle per account. Off-session PaymentIntents on the saved card, every attempt in `GameCharge`, idempotency key = cycle + kind + attempt; a charge taken with no pack to give is refunded. Payment Links are retired (`retireOldLinks`); the webhook only matches old links and membership events.
- **Price rule** (`suggest` + `shopPrice` in `lib/pricing/engine.ts`): one headline per source (sold-comp median, TCGplayer market, Scryfall ask, eBay active median), then the median of those; one source = that number; none = $1. Round to the nearest dollar, min $1; under $1 stays exact. The game never prices a card: pack value = sum of the cards' existing `listPrice`. Sources and disagreement are stored on the card, never shown on the shop (the disclaimers cover it).
- **Collection + shipping** (`lib/game/ship.ts`, `/collection`, `/api/play/collection/:id`, `/api/play/ship/*`, `lib/ship/easypost.ts`): buying a pack never ships it or charges shipping. A kept or blind pack is stored on the account (`GamePack.orderId` null) and listed in the Collection tab by category, date and pack number; tap one for its 12 framed cards with tilt, Exit back to the list. The Ship button at the bottom: pick one or more stored packs → confirm (or edit) the address (EasyPost-verified) → one USPS Ground Advantage rate for one combined parcel (`parcelFor(n)`: 6×4 in, 4 oz + 1.5 oz per extra pack, +1 in height per 4 packs; `ShipQuote.packIds`, 30 min) → Stripe charges shipping only (`GameCharge.kind` ship, `ref` = parcel) → one label for the selection (`labels/<order>.pdf`, tracking emailed via Resend). Never auto-ship. EasyPost down → flat $5.95 and I buy the label from the order. From 1424 Orchid Lane, Kissimmee, FL 34744, one service only. The member mailer credit zeros one parcel per period.
- **Owners, payouts, reserve** (`lib/partners/*`, `/admin/partners` "Payouts", `/consign`): every card is tagged before it can enter a bin: a founder (`Card.partnerId`: Matthew, Adrian, Mike) or a consignment sender (`Card.senderId`, `Sender`). Tag on upload (`createBatch(name, "f:<id>" | "s:<id>")`, required), per batch, or per card. Stacks copy `partnerIds` / `senderIds`; packs mix owners. On a completed keep or blind buy (`recordSale`): founders split pack price (keep $2.99, blind $4.99; `KEEP_SPLIT_INCLUDES_REVEAL` false) − Stripe fee − stamp credit by engine value (`splitNet`, leftover cent to the largest share) → `PartnerEarning`; each consignment card owes its sender its engine price (`SenderEarning`, reserved → payable), not a share; the founders' unpaid remainder is company money → `ReserveEntry` "pack". The reserve (`ReserveEntry` ledger) is filled only by company money: $1 peeks (`reservePeek`, refunds reversed), membership invoices (`invoice.paid` webhook), the company part of packs, founder shares retained (`payPartner(id, {retain})`); sender payouts take from it. A stack with consignment cards is built only if `reserveNow().headroom` (on hand − payable − consignment already in stacks) covers all of them (`fitsReserve`); otherwise they wait in the hold bin. Payouts are manual Stripe Connect transfers of the payable balance. **Consignment is locked by default** (`Settings.consignOpen`): `/consign` shows Coming soon with no form, `/api/consign` refuses, admin can't accept requests, add senders, tag sender cards or pay senders, and the builder skips sender cards; locking dissolves ready stacks holding them. Shipping is never split; unsold cards and expired peeks pay nothing.
- **Pack desk** (`/admin/lil-stack`, `lib/desk.ts`, `/api/admin/desk`), steps in order on the page: 1 Scan a batch, 2 Sort into bins by the location code, 3 Build when the mix is ready, 4 Pull the sheet, 5 Confirm the pack. Every desk card gets a slot code per category (`Card.location`, e.g. `B-14`: tray A bulk, B mid, C top, D hit, H hold) and only cards a founder marked placed (`sortedAt`) can be packed. Columns Bulk / Mid / Top / Hit / Hold with upright thumbnails. Hold = needs a look, sideways (one-tap ↻ ↕ ↺ ✓ via `/api/admin/cards/:id/rotate?deg=`), no owner, no price, chase while off, consignment. "Build next 10" shows only when 10 legal packs can be built (`previewBuild`), else one line says what's missing; built packs are `pulling` until **Confirm** puts them on sale (`confirmPack`, members' first hour) and marks their cards pulled. A pulled pack that comes back (pass, dissolve) lists its cards under step 2 to put back. The pull sheet (`/admin/packs/:id`) lists the 12 slot codes in big type in tray order. The desk shows the demo-card count (must be 0). No seed or demo data: real scans only.
- **Facebook sales** (pack desk, `sellOnFacebook` / `voidFacebookSale` in `lib/game/packs.ts`, `/api/admin/desk` actions `facebook`, `facebook-void`): the founder picks a category and marks a Facebook sale; the next ready pack is reserved like a blind buy (same queue, members' first hour ignored), leaves the site queue, its cards go Sold (`soldChannel` facebook) and the desk shows "Pull Pokemon Pack 12" with the pull sheet. `GamePack.status` `sold-facebook`. An empty category does nothing and says so. Never read Marketplace automatically. A sold pack returns to ready only when the founder voids that Facebook sale. No price is captured, so no `recordSale` split runs for it.
- **Ingest is two steps** (`lib/ingestQueue.ts`): an upload (images, flatbed crops, PDFs) only stores the files and answers at once ("19 pages received.", `/api/admin/batches/:id/queue`). The work runs in the background, a unit at a time (`tick`): for each new file make its card → for each card `processCard` (orient → frame → identify the upright crop only → price → stock / Needs a look), so a card is in its bin as soon as it's priced. Ticks come from the open desk / batch page (`IngestTicker`, "4 of 19 priced") and from the scheduled Netlify function `netlify/functions/ingest-tick.mts` every minute (`x-ingest-key` = sha256 of `INGEST_KEY` or the admin password). One worker per batch (`ingestLockUntil`). A card that fails once is flagged to Needs a look, never retried in a loop.
- **PDFs** (browser split: `lib/client/pdfSplit.ts`, server: `lib/pdf.ts`, `lib/pdfIngest.ts`): the admin's browser renders each page at 300 dpi with pdf.js (`public/vendor/pdfjs-<ver>/`, copied by `scripts/copy-opencv.mjs`); several cards → the flatbed detector (opencv.js) crops each; one card → the page is uploaded whole with `meta.trim` and the server's `trimToCard` (sharp) crops to the card's straight bounding box (border-strip background; a card-shaped page is kept whole; never turned a few degrees). Never put mupdf, opencv or other WASM on the server: the Netlify server function must stay under the 50 MB Lambda limit (it is about 49 MB). Each crop is a front scan tagged to the batch owner. A page with no card edge is kept whole and flagged (`UploadFile.flag` → `holdReason` "PDF page N…" → Needs a look); blank pages are noted; unreadable pages recorded (`/api/admin/batches/:id/pdf/:pdfId`). The same PDF (sha-256, `PdfIngest.hash`) is never ingested twice. The batch page shows "12 cards from this PDF, 2 need a look (pages 3, 7)". Orientation is the usual 90° steps by the vision model with the upright rule (name/HP on top, art under it, text below; full art: set number at the bottom corner; ignore holo); unsure → Hold for a one-tap rotate.
- **Identify**: CardSight AI (`lib/sources/sportsCatalog.ts`, `CARDSIGHT_API_KEY`, server only) identifies sports and Pokemon scans and gives the Pokemon energy type.
- Public pages never list stock. A player sees card names, set, engine price and front image only for a pack reserved to, kept by, or sold to them (`packView`, `/api/play/image/:id` checks `reservedBy`). Every pack page shows the four disclaimers (`lib/disclaimers.ts`).
- Copy: titles plain; descriptions short and friendly (`friendly()` in `lib/listing/templates.ts` drops any sentence with investment, gem-mint or guarantee talk).
- **Pack builder** (`lib/game/rules.ts` pure, `lib/game/packs.ts` DB), per category, never mixed. Bins: bulk < $0.25, mid $0.25–$0.99, top $1–$3.99 (usually $1–$2), hit $4–$9.99, chase ≥ $10; member stack swaps its top slot for a better $2.01–$3.99 card. Base pack (about 80 in 100): 7 bulk + 4 mid + 1 top, summed $3.20–$3.80 (close to the $3.99 keep), no card over $3.99. Hit pack: 7 bulk + 4 mid + exactly one hit card, $5–$12. Chase pack: 8 bulk + 3 mid + a chase card (flag on only). A draw outside its band is thrown out and drawn again; a slot the bins can't fill falls back to base or leaves the category closed (never pad). Mix (`nextKind`) over the last 100 built packs: 80 base / 18 hit / 2 chase; every built pack counts (a kept hit = a sold hit); no new hits while hits are over 20% of the last 100 or over a fifth of the ready queue. Packs are built only from the **pack desk** (`/admin/lil-stack`, `lib/desk.ts`): never automatically after a batch, save, reprice or pass. A pack is legal only with no duplicate card, at most two of one Pokémon type (`Card.cardType`), at most two cards of one player (`packProblem`; illegal draws are thrown out and the reason is shown). A purchase only reserves an existing pack, at random, one queue for peek and blind. `GamePack.status`: pulling (built, being pulled, not for sale) | available | reserved | kept | expired | sold-blind | sold-facebook | dissolved; `kind`: base | hit | chase | member. A card edit dissolves its available pack; reserved/sold packs and their prices are frozen. Pull sheet per pack at `/admin/packs/:id` (CHASE / HIT marked, location note). Card status `LilStack` = in a pack. `category` comes from `categorize()`; a manual category sticks.
- **Chase cards** (≥ $10 engine price, `chaseList`): flag per category in Settings `chaseOn`, default off; can only be turned on once that category has one scanned (`setChase`, Admin → Packs; off dissolves ready chase packs). Off: no $10+ card in any pack, and the odds say "Chase cards are not in packs until that feature is turned on". On: about 2 in 100 built packs are chase packs (`CHASE_RESERVE_CAP` = 1 reserved per category at once), and the odds say "About 2 in 100 packs contain a card priced at $10 or more." The hit line "About 18 in 100 packs contain a card priced from $4 to $10." is always shown.
- **Membership** (`lib/game/member.ts`, `/play/member`): optional $7.99/month Stripe subscription (price by lookup key `trade_shark_member_monthly`, made on first join). Perks only: no midnight lockout, one mailer credit per billing period (zeros one parcel), one member stack per period (a base pack with its top slot swapped for a $2–$4 card at reservation, used once shown), and new drops (batch / Draw packs builds, `publicAt`) members-only for the first hour. It never adds reveals, cheaper packs, or free shipping on every pack. Status re-read from Stripe hourly (`refreshMember`), webhook events speed it up. Perks are rows in `MemberPerk` per period.
- **Card presentation** (`lib/present.ts`): show the real scan, never art. On upload (after auto-rotate) and lazily on first view: straighten a small skew (min-bounding-box search, ≤ 10°), trim the scanner background, frame on a white rounded border with a thin inner edge and soft shadow (PNG, `Card.frontDisplay`); scaled down only, never blurred or redrawn. Hand rotation clears it. In the game (`TiltCard`): drag tilts rotateX/rotateY only, capped 8°, highlight follows the drag, snaps back on release; works in the grid and the enlarged view; pointer/touch only, no gyroscope, no flip, no back; off under reduced motion.
- **Brand look: a quiet card counter.** Cream paper (`bg-sand`), navy ink, one gold edge; plain large type; the pack is the only object with a shadow (`.pack-shadow`). The shark is only the small fin mark (`SharkFin`, navy fin on a gold line) in the cream header and on the pack. No halftone, lightning, comic bursts, stickers, pattern tiles, looping glow or dark crypto glow. Page titles are `PageTitle` (navy, thin gold rule, the rules sentence under it in navy). Art comes from Gemini (`scripts/gemini-art.mjs`, run by hand with `GEMINI_API_KEY`; the site never calls Gemini at runtime): the sealed shark pack per category (`public/brand/pack-*.webp`) and one quiet stage per category (`scenes`: baseball leather + seam, football one yard line on turf, Pokemon flat violet with a soft arcade light; darkened, empty centre; no text, characters or league/Pokémon/Nintendo/Disney marks; `lib/brandScenes.json` lists what exists, a missing one stays plain navy). The stage sits behind the pack only (home tiles and the game), with the category name and "$1 to reveal." under it on cream. Motion is CSS only: the reveal button (`btn-reveal`, navy with a gold edge) dips on press; after a payment the seal splits open (`seal-top`/`seal-body`, ~0.5 s) then the cards fan (`after-seal .card-in`); the 10 s / 20 s nudge is a small tilt of the pack (`ls-nudge`), never the page; Keep (`btn-keep`) is green and still; Pass is plain text; a passed pack stays faded. All off under reduced motion. No video clips.
- **Home** picks a category (Baseball / Football / Pokemon Pack, open or "Restocking") and shows the rules sentence. `/shop` and `/lil-stack` redirect there; `/card/:id` is a 404. The wow score (`lib/wow.ts`) is an admin-only tag.
- Delight (`lib/client/feel.ts`): one short pop on primary buttons, confetti on a keep or blind buy. Respect the Mute toggle and prefers-reduced-motion (nudge and seal split turn off); never block a save on it.
- **Add price/identity sources as adapters** in `lib/sources/` (`IdentifyAdapter` / `PriceAdapter` in `lib/sources/types.ts`), registered in `lib/sources/index.ts`. Adapters fail soft: return `{ status, reason }`, never throw, and skip cleanly when their env key is missing.
- Keep junk out of medians: comps go through `filterComps` (excluded rows are kept and labeled, not deleted). Retail asks (Scryfall, eBay active listings) are their own headlines, never mixed into sold comps.
- Every quote is persisted (`PriceQuote`); only the latest fetch per source drives the suggestion. Manual override always wins.
- Confidence is stored (and highlighted in review) but no longer gates publishing; only a missing name holds a card.
- Scans are private: serve them only via `/api/admin/images/*` (auth) or `/api/play/image/:id` (the framed scan, only for the player who holds or bought that pack). Labels only via `/api/admin/orders/:id/label`.
- No league, Pokémon, Nintendo, Disney, or other third-party logos in the brand. Colors: navy `#0B1F3A`, cream/sand `#F4EFE6`, gold `#D9A441`, teal `#1AA6A6`, coral `#E85D4C`, white; one sport accent per category (`ACCENT`: baseball leather `#9E2B25`, football turf `#2F7D3A`, Pokemon arcade `#FFD23F`). Quiet card counter, not a bank and not a comic: no stock gradients, no AI glow.
- Data: Supabase Postgres, schema `trade_shark` (Prisma), and the private `trade-shark-scans` bucket via `lib/storage.ts`. Hosting: Netlify — keep each request short (one card per process call) and send large uploads straight to Storage.
- **Auto-orientation** (`lib/orient.ts`, the first step of `processBatch`, one card per request): every crop is stood upright before identify/price/publish. The vision model says which image edge is the card's top, then re-checks the turned image; the same turn goes to the back; the fixed image is saved over the crop (original kept only for a guess). Without a vision key: landscape crops get a layout guess and wait in Needs a look; portrait is never flipped. Anything still sideways, an unsure vision answer, or a portrait card the layout cue thinks is upside down → `holdReason: "rotation"` → Hold tray on the desk for a one-tap rotate, never published sideways or on a guess. Cards scanned before auto-rotate are re-checked one per call via `/api/admin/cards/reorient`. Each crop is turned on its own, never the whole sheet. Hand rotation (flatbed "Fallback" panel, queue ↻ buttons, `/api/admin/cards/:id/rotate`) is admin fallback only.
- Flatbed split runs client-side with opencv.js (`lib/flatbed/*`, served from `public/vendor` by `scripts/copy-opencv.mjs`). Never send a whole flatbed sheet to a vision API to split it. Front/back crops pair only by sheet position with identical layouts (`checkSheetPairing`); never guess pairs.
- Checks: `npm test` and `npm run lint` (tsc). Schema changes: edit `prisma/schema.prisma`, run `npx prisma db push` (uses `DIRECT_URL`).
